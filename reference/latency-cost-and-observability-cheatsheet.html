Latency, cost, and observability

A quick, practical guide to making this engineering decision.

中文 用于本课工程决策的证据速查表。

One-line rule

Average latency can hide a slow tail that makes the system unusable in a pilot.

一句话规则：平均延迟可能掩盖让系统在试点中不可用的慢尾延迟。

Core terms

TermMeaning
p50 and p95The median and the slow tail. Both matter because a fast average can hide painful outliers.
Cost driversEmbedding calls, graph LLM calls, answer generation, judge calls, and context size.
ObservabilityStructured traces, metrics, and logs that show what happened without exposing secrets or document text.

Evidence card

NeedRecordDon't claim
BaselineCommand, configuration, observed resultThe baseline is a target.
ChangeOne hypothesis or decisionYou can tell what caused the result when several things changed.
MetricUse p50/p95 or per-question latency, result counts, and context size; never log secrets or raw sensitive content.One metric tells you everything about quality.
SafetyA failure and a regression caseA fluent answer proves the system is reliable.

证据卡：记录基线、一次改动、指标和安全回归；不要把单一指标等同于整体质量。
