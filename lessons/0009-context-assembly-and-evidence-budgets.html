Lesson 09 · 25–35 minutes

Context assembly and evidence budgets

Retrieved results don’t walk straight into the model’s context—ordering, truncation, and duplication can quietly strip out the evidence you worked hard to find. Here’s how to catch it before it costs you an answer.

中文 检索到的结果不会原封不动地进到模型的上下文里——排序、截断、重复，随便一个都可能在你没留神的时候，把辛苦找到的证据悄悄挤掉。这节课教你怎么在丢证据之前就发现问题。

本课的具体收获： 画一份多跳问题的证据存活地图，再给出一条站得住脚、有边界的上下文预算建议。

1 · The skill

Take q30 or q63 and follow it end to end—from the sources retrieval dug up, through context assembly, all the way to the prompt budget.

  • Context assembly: the step that turns backend results into the actual text the answering model reads.
  • Evidence budget: the ceiling on how much context you can afford, set by character count, tokens, latency, or cost.
  • Source survival: whether the evidence an answer actually needs is still standing after formatting and truncation have had their say.

Primary reading: Anthropic — Contextual Retrieval

上下文组装：把后端检索到的结果，变成真正喂给回答模型看的那段文字。证据预算：你能负担得起的上下文上限，由字符数、token 数、延迟或成本说了算。来源存活率：格式化、截断这些操作做完之后，某条证据是不是还留在最终拼出来的上下文里。

Mission connection

A system can’t cite evidence the model never saw. This is exactly where solid retrieval quietly turns into a partial answer, or a hallucinated one.

模型压根没看到的证据，系统没法引用。检索明明做对了，答案却可能就在上下文组装这一步，悄悄变成半吊子回答，甚至是幻觉。
